# CFPB Chinese-English financial glossary

The source is the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau's March 2024 *Chinese-English glossary of financial terms* ([official PDF](https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_adult-fin-ed_chinese-style-guide-glossary.pdf)). The Bureau says it created and uses this glossary to translate consumer-education material and is sharing it for public use. CFPB's [website policy](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/privacy/website-privacy-policy/) states that material created by the Bureau is public domain unless otherwise noted; third-party material, if identified in the source, remains subject to its own terms. Attribution: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB), March 2024.

## Changes in this project

- A text extraction of the official 81-page PDF is retained at `sources/cfpb/cfpb-financial-glossary-2024.md` for repeatable review. Its SHA-256 is `e319ee28ae2a0a3a36995ec437386f9813ed2a67b6367def90447a60a3c7bbda` (111,291 bytes). The source of authority remains the CFPB PDF; extraction metadata is not treated as a CFPB publication date.
- Traditional Chinese text was converted to Simplified Chinese with the project's pinned OpenCC build tool. Candidate pairs were kept only when the Chinese form is an exact local pinyin key, the English head has local UK or US IPA, and ECDICT independently contains the same Chinese gloss and part of speech.
- Of 249 qualifying candidate rows, 246 are unique Chinese-English pairs. There are 223 existing or newly selected mappings among those pairs (90.65% of this filtered subset); 67 new mappings were explicitly reviewed and selected, including 13 new English headwords. Examples include `违约 → default`, `透支 → overdraft`, `汇款 → remittance`, `受托人 → fiduciary`, and `公用事业 → utilities`. The remaining candidate rows include 158 exact mappings already present, two keys at the expansion limit, and 22 candidates whose finance-specific sense was not individually adjudicated; the latter are marked deferred rather than presented as approved additions. This rate describes only the subset with local pinyin, IPA, and an exact ECDICT gloss; it is not coverage of the full CFPB glossary. The evidence file records the glossary line, source wording, local pinyin/frequency, IPA availability, ECDICT row, existing mappings, decision, and reason.
- The source contributed 211 unique finance-glossary membership tags; 193 are new business-category memberships after merging existing business sources. These tags mean that the word occurs in this consumer-finance glossary, not that CFPB certifies it as a general business term.
- Runtime data contains only the short reviewed mapping rows and compact membership rows. The glossary text, longer explanations, and candidate audit are not searched while typing.
