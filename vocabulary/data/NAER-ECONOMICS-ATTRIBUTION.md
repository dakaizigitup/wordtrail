# National Academy for Educational Research Economics Terminology

The source snapshot is the National Academy for Educational Research's
*Economics Terminology* dataset (dataset 15405), published 2015-05-22 and
updated by the Taiwan Open Data Platform on 2025-12-16. The CSV is retrieved
from the resource linked by the [official dataset page](https://data.gov.tw/en/datasets/15405).

The provider releases the data under the [Open Government Data License,
version 1.0](https://data.gov.tw/license). The license allows reproduction,
distribution, adaptation, and sublicensing for any purpose, subject to its
attribution requirement. Attribution for this project:

> National Academy for Educational Research, 2025, *Economics Terminology*
> (dataset 15405, updated 2025-12-16). Released under the Open Government Data
> License, version 1.0. Source: https://data.gov.tw/en/datasets/15405.

The project converts source Traditional Chinese to Simplified Chinese for
candidate matching, then retains only individually reviewed, complete
English-Chinese mappings with local IPA, an exact ECDICT noun gloss, an
existing pinyin key, and room in the per-key expansion limit. It does not
redistribute source definitions or examples. The complete CSV snapshot,
metadata, hash, and row-level decisions are kept in this repository for
reproducibility; only accepted compact mappings and category tags enter the
runtime lookup.
