# 词伴暖色小书灵

用户要求：软件图标改为暖色、可爱一点。

模式：内置 imagegen，单张新图生成。未使用 CLI/API fallback。生成原图保存在 `wordtrail-warm-master.png`，平台导出图为 `wordtrail-warm-icon.png`。`scripts/export_icons.ps1` 仅按平台尺寸机械导出，不改画面内容；Android Launcher 使用自适应安全区，iOS AppIcon 为 1024×1024。

## 最终生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE production-ready square mobile app icon artwork for a friendly Chinese vocabulary-learning keyboard app called Wordtrail (词伴). Design an original cute little open-book mascot: two plump rounded cream-peach pages form ONE simple soft silhouette, a gentle central book fold, two small cocoa-brown oval eyes, a tiny curved smile, two subtle coral blush circles. A warm apricot/coral outer book cover and a small rounded page-tab suggest reading. Friendly, calm, modern kawaii, more tasteful than childish. Warm palette only: vanilla cream background, soft apricot, peach coral and cocoa brown. Clean flat vector-like illustration with precise smooth edges and only restrained soft shading, no grain, no complicated scene. Full bleed SOLID vanilla-cream background across the entire square; DO NOT pre-round the square corners and do not place the icon inside a device mockup. Mascot centered horizontally and vertically, approximately 58 percent of the square's width, all essential features comfortably inside the central 60 percent square, roomy uniform surrounding cream margin so both circular and squircle launcher masks remain safe. Compact coherent silhouette easily readable at 48px. No text, no Chinese characters, no letters, no watermark, no gradients across the background, no extra objects, no border, no app screenshot. Deliver a square 1024x1024 or higher artwork.

## 目视检查

生成原图 1254×1254：书本、眼睛、微笑和腮红清晰，暖色调符合要求，没有文字和水印。实际书本占宽约 73%，高于提示中的 58%，因此 Android 前景增加 16.67% 边距，确保圆形等启动器遮罩不裁掉书本。背景带轻微柔和明暗，导出保留原画。最终导出和启动器效果需结合实测截图检查。
